<Steps>

  <Step title="Consultants Import and Management">
    - Effortlessly **import and export all consultants** in bulk with just a few clicks.  
    - Manage consultant data such as **full name, email address, and phone number** directly from the dashboard.  
    - Clicking on a consultant’s name opens a **detailed profile** showing:  
      - Email, phone number, job title  
      - Total experience  
      - Address summary  
      - Education and certifications  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff1.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff2.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="📋 Suitable Jobs">
        - View all **jobs suitable for this consultant**.  
        - Apply filters to find the most relevant job openings.  
        - Send job details directly via **SMS, email, or call**.  
        - Easily manage consultant–job matching to speed up placements.  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff2a.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="🗒️ Notes">
        - Maintain consultant-specific **notes, tasks, timelines, and documents**.  
        - Keep track of recruiter interactions and progress in one place.  
        - Enhance coordination across the staffing team with context-based updates. 
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff2b.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Requirement Tab">
    - View and manage all **job requirements** in a unified dashboard.  
    - **Import or export** job data effortlessly.  
    - Clicking any job reveals its full details:
      - Job title, duration, type, and shift (day/night)  
      - Status (open/closed) and start date  
      - Job specialty, bill rate, and complete description  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3a.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />



    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="👥 Suitable Consultants">
        - Displays all **consultants suitable for this job**.  
        - View contact info (phone, email, address) directly.  
        - Click a consultant’s name to view complete details — experience, education, and history.  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3b.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="🗒️ Notes">
        - Add or review **job-related notes** shared by recruiters or managers.  
        - Track updates, follow-ups, and collaboration comments.  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3c.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="💰 Rates">
        - View detailed **rate and allowance breakdowns**, including:  
          - Weekly non-taxable allowance  
          - Housing allowance  
          - Total hourly rate and bill rate  
          - Pay per hour  
          - Shift and overtime rates (extended rates, from–to values)  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3d.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="🔗 Apply Link">
        - Provides a direct **application link** where consultants can apply to this job.  
        - Useful for tracking job applications submitted via the consultant portal.  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff3e.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Job Board Settings">
    - Configure your **company branding and job board appearance**.  
    - Upload your **company logo** and define your **career page URL slug** (your hosted job board link). 
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff4.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
 
    - Add all relevant links:  
      - LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram  
      - About Us, Contact Us        
    - Assign a **chat assistant** to respond to consultant inquiries.  
    - Configure **email settings**:  
      - Default From, To, CC, and BCC email addresses  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff4a.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />

    - Customize sections for your job board:
      1. **Why Choose Us** — Add image, title, and description highlighting company strengths.
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff5.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />  
      2. **Benefits** — Showcase employee or consultant benefits with images and short blurbs. 
      3. **Testimonials** — Upload consultant feedback with images and descriptions.  
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff6.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
    - Includes a **live preview** of your configured job board.
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff7.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />





  </Step>

  <Step title="Logs Tab">
    - View detailed **system logs** for all staffing activities.  
    - Tracks:
      - Consultant imports/exports  
      - Job updates  
      - Messages and task assignments  
    - Every action is **timestamped** and linked to a user for accountability.  
    - Filter and search logs for quick insights.  
    - Ensures a **transparent and auditable workflow**.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff8.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
  </Step>

   <Step title="Job Application Tab">
    - On this page, you can view all applications received through the job apply URL, along with key details such as the applicant’s information, applied position, and submission status.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Staffing/staff9.jpg" alt="Consultant management interface" />
  </Step>

</Steps>
