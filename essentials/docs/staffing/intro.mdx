<Steps>
  <Step title="Consultants Import and Management">
    - Effortlessly **import and export consultants** in bulk with just a few clicks.  
    - The system automatically **extracts and maps consultant details**, including experience, skills, and certifications.  
    - Once imported, the AI-powered matcher links consultants to **relevant job descriptions** based on their profiles.  
    - Recruiters can **send follow-ups instantly** — via SMS, email, or even direct calls — for shortlisted consultants.  
    - Clicking on a consultant’s name opens a detailed view with **personal info, work history, and job compatibility insights**.  
    - From the same panel, you can **assign tasks**, track actions, and update consultant statuses in real time.  
    - The entire process ensures **better consultant visibility** and faster recruitment decisions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Requirement Tab">
    - This section displays all **current job listings** in one centralized view.  
    - By clicking any job, recruiters can instantly see **suitable consultants** matched to that requirement.  
    - Jobs can be **imported or exported** to streamline data handling and third-party integrations.  
    - Each job card provides **key metadata** such as title, skills, experience level, and location filters for easy access.  
    - Enables smooth coordination between **hiring teams and consultants**, reducing manual effort and turnaround time.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Job Board Settings">
    - The **Job Board Settings** tab manages everything related to job postings and company branding.  
    - You can configure **social media links, logos, and company details** to ensure professional consistency across job platforms.  
    - Integrates with external boards for **multi-channel posting** and better reach.  
    - Helps recruiters maintain a **clean and modern brand identity** when sharing or displaying jobs publicly.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Logs Tab">
    - A dedicated area to **monitor all system activity logs** in real time.  
    - Every action — imports, exports, updates, messages, and assignments — is **timestamped and recorded** for transparency.  
    - Useful for **audit tracking**, accountability, and performance insights.  
    - Smart filters and search allow quick navigation to any event or user action.  
    - Ensures a **secure, traceable, and reliable** workflow for the staffing process.
