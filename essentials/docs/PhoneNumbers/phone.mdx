Learn how to manage and configure phone numbers in ContactSwing. This guide shows you how to handle your communication channels effectively.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View and manage your phone numbers">
    See a list of all purchased phone numbers, including:
    - **Number**: The phone number in international format (e.g., +1XXXXXXXX).
    - **From**: The provider (e.g., Twilio).
    - **Status**: Toggle each number between active and inactive.

    You can activate or deactivate any number by clicking the switch in the "Status" column.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/p1.jpg" alt="Phone number list" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a new phone number">
    To acquire a new number, select **Buy Phone Number**. A window will display available numbers with details like region, postal code, and purchase price. Choose your preferred number and click **Purchase** to add it to your account.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/p4.jpg" alt="Buy phone number" />
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/p2.jpg" alt="Available numbers" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify a phone number">
    Click **Verify Your Number** to access the verification form. Enter the contact's name and phone number, then click **Verify** to confirm their details.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/p6.jpg" alt="Verify phone number" />
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/p3.jpg" alt="Verification form" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Additional notes

*   Phone number charges are billed on a monthly basis.
*   Effectively manage multiple numbers to support diverse communication requirements.
*   The process for purchasing and verifying numbers is straightforward, making it suitable for businesses with extensive client interactions.
