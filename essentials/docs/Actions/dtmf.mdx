## Step-by-step guide

Follow these steps to create your DTMF action:

### 1. Configure your DTMF action

Fill in the required fields:
- **Name**: Enter a name for your action (e.g., "My DTMF Action").
- **Action Timing**: Select your Action Timing
- **Description**: Briefly describe your DTMF action (e.g., "Action for sending automated DTMF signals").


After filling in all fields, click **Create Action** to save your DTMF action. 🎉

### 2. View and update your DTMF action

You can view your DTMF Action on the Actions screen. To update, select the DTMF Action, make your changes, and click **Update Actions**.
