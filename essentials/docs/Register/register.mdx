## Step-by-step guide
 
Follow these steps to sign up for ContactSwing:
 
<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Click **Start Free Trial** on the ContactSwing registration page.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s1.jpg" alt="Start Free Trial" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Provide your email address and create a secure password.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s2.jpg" alt="Enter Email and Password" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Upon submitting your information, locate the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your email and enter it to confirm your email address.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s3.jpg" alt="Verify Email" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Following successful email verification, you will be directed to the sign-in page. Use your email and password to log in to your account.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s4.jpg" alt="Sign In" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your registration">
    After entering the necessary details, click **Register**. This will take you to the subscription selection page.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s5.jpg" alt="Complete Registration" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Choose the subscription plan that best aligns with your requirements. Be sure to check for any available special offers or discounts.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/s6.jpg" alt="Choose a Plan" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize registration">
    Complete the payment process to finalize your registration and gain access to the ContactSwing AI Voice Assistant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🎉 Congratulations!

You have successfully registered! Your ContactSwing AI Voice Assistant is ready to help you improve sales, operations, and support with no-code AI calling.
