## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
    <Step title="Set up your Airtable trigger">
        - **Trigger:** New or updated Record
        - **Connection:** `Airtable`
        - **Base:** Select the base where you’ll be adding or updating records.
        - **Table:** Select the table where you’ll be adding or updating records.
        - **Trigger Field:** Choose the field that will trigger the flow.
        - **View:** Grid view or Gallery view.
        - Every time you add or update a record, this trigger will activate the flow.

        <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/a1.jpg" alt="Workflow" />
    </Step>
    <Step title="Configure the Contactswing Call action">
        - In Contactswing, choose **CALL** as the response type.
        - Enter your **Workspace API Key**.
        - Add the **Webhook URL** provided by Contactswing.
        - Whenever a new or updated record is added in Airtable, Contactswing will automatically place a phone call using the configured details.

        <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/a2.jpg" alt="Workflow" />
    </Step>
    <Step title="Activate your Agentic AI Flow">
        - Review all trigger and action settings.
        - Click **Publish** to activate the flow.
        - Once published, the system will automatically trigger the phone call whenever a new or updated record is added in Airtable.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Example scenario

- A customer’s details are added or updated in your Airtable base.
- This change triggers the Agentic AI flow.
- Contactswing automatically initiates a phone call to the customer with the configured message or agent interaction.

## Benefits

- **Automation:** No need for manual dialing or monitoring.
- **Timely responses:** Customers receive instant, personalized calls.
- **Customizable:** Adapt the call content and workflow to your business needs.
