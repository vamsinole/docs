Learn how to connect and use Zoho Bigin with ContactSwing. This guide shows you how to set up and leverage the integration effectively.

# Step-by-step guide

Follow these steps to add the Zoho Bigin integration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Integrations section">
    Locate and select the **Integrations** section within your application. This area lets you connect with various external services to enhance your AI Agents' capabilities. You see a list of integrations available for connection.

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Integration/Zoho/zoho1.jpg" alt="Integrations" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Zoho Bigin and connect your account">
    Click the **Zoho Bigin** integration option to proceed with connecting your Zoho Bigin account. This enables you to use the Zoho Bigin account while creating agents. You need to provide your Zoho Bigin username and password.
    - **API Key**: Enter your Zoho Bigin username and password.

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Integration/Zoho/zoho2.jpg" alt="Zoho Bigin API" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize the integration">
    After you enter the required details (API key and description), click **Connect** to finalize the integration with your Zoho Bigin account. ✨
  </Step>
</Steps>
