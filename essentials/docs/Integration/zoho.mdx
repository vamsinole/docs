## Overview

Set up Zoho Bigin with ContactSwing to run multi-channel campaigns from within your CRM and leverage AI agents.

  <video src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/Videos/Integration/ZohoBegin/zoho-integration.mp4" autoplay playsinline controls title="Zoho Bigin Integration"></video>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations section">
    Locate and select the **Integrations** section within ContactSwing. This area lets you connect external services to enhance AI Agents' capabilities.

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Integration/Zoho/zoho1.jpg" alt="Integrations section" />
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Zoho Bigin">
    Click **Zoho Bigin** and enter your credentials.
    - **Username/Password**: Use your Zoho Bigin login details (API authentication).

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Integration/Zoho/zoho2.jpg" alt="Zoho Bigin connection" />
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Finalize connection">
    Click **Connect** to complete the integration. ✨
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run Campaigns from Zoho Bigin

Install ContactSwing app from Zoho Marketplace, then execute campaigns directly:

<div class="card-video">
  <video src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/Videos/Integration/ZohoBegin/zohobigin-app.mp4" autoplay playsinline controls title="Run campaigns from Zoho Bigin"></video>
</div>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install ContactSwing app">
    In Zoho Bigin: **Settings** → **Marketplace** → Search **ContactSwing** → **Install**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Run campaigns">
    1. Go to **Contacts** and select contact(s).  
    2. Click **ContactSwing** in the actions menu.  
    3. Choose channel: **Call**, **SMS**, **Email**, or **WhatsApp**.  
    4. Select **campaign**, **touchpoint**, and **assistant**.  
    5. Fill details and **initiate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Example Flow:**  
1. Connect Zoho Bigin in ContactSwing **Integrations**.  
2. In Zoho Bigin, select contacts → **ContactSwing**.  
3. Pick pre-built campaign → Execute.

## Key Features

- **Campaign execution**: Multi-channel (calls, SMS, email, WhatsApp) from Zoho Bigin UI.
- **Bulk outreach**: Mass SMS, emails, WhatsApp, auto-dial for promotions/follow-ups.
- **Single actions**: Quick calls/SMS/emails per contact.
- **No data import**: Data stays in Zoho Bigin; ContactSwing executes actions only.
- **Campaign import**: Design in ContactSwing, run from Zoho Bigin.
