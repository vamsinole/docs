<Steps>
  <Step title="Accessing the Email Inbox">
    To view your email records, access the **Email Inbox** area. Here, you can examine replies created by the AI Agent and keep track of automated responses handled by AI email tools. 🤖
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Inbox/EMAIL/email1.jpg" alt="email Inbox" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Search Email in the inbox">
    Utilize the search bar within the Inbox to locate specific Email.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Inbox/EMAIL/email2.jpg" alt="email Inbox" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use filters to find Email logs">
   Filter your Email using these options:
    - **From:** Enter the number from which you received the Email.
    - **To:** Input the destination number of the Email.
    - **Session ID:** Retrieve logs by entering the session ID.
    - **AI Agent:** Filter entries by selecting a particular agent.
    - **Date:** Filter by date range (From and To).
    - **Campaign:** Filter by campaign name.
    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Inbox/EMAIL/email3.jpg" alt="email Inbox" />
  </Step>

</Steps>

### Additional Capabilities

The AI Email Management Interface enables you to:

- Optimize automation workflows for timely, accurate communication.
- Enhance engagement using AI-powered messaging for sales, support, and outreach.

This system is ideal for businesses managing Email-based customer support, sales assistants, and other AI-driven messaging solutions. ✨
