Learn about ContactSwing's knowledge base system and how it helps power your AI agents. This guide provides an overview of knowledge base features and capabilities.

### 📁 Create a Knowledge Base
Organize and structure your essential documents by creating a dedicated knowledge base. This keeps information easily accessible for your team and AI agents.

[Learn how to create a Knowledge Base here.](../knowledge-base/createkb)

---

### ❓ Add FAQs
Improve the user experience and make information readily available by adding frequently asked questions (FAQs) and their answers.


---

### 📥 Add Files
Ensure your knowledge base is always current and comprehensive by uploading documents, resources, and any other relevant files directly.


---

### 🔗 Add URLs
Seamlessly integrate important web resources into your knowledge base, making them easily accessible for both your team and AI agents.


---

By using these features, you create a robust knowledge base that supports information sharing and improves user engagement.
