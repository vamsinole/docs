---
title: Multi-Tenant Agency Configuration
---

Set up and manage your agency environment by configuring branding, domains, pricing, customers, and module access. This allows you to onboard multiple customers under your own brand while maintaining full control from a single platform.

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>

  <Step title="General Settings">
    Configure your agency’s basic branding and legal information.

    - **Name**: Enter your agency name  
    - **Custom Logo**: Upload your brand logo  
    - **Favicon Logo**: Upload a favicon for browser tabs  
    - **Privacy Policy URL**: Add your privacy policy link  
    - **Terms & Conditions URL**: Add your terms page  
    - **Website URL**: Enter your agency website  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency1.png" alt="Agency General Settings" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Custom Domain Setup">
    Configure a custom domain for white-label access.

    - **Domain Name**: Enter your custom domain  
    - Ensure the **SSL certificate** is generated and active  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency2.png" alt="Custom Domain Setup" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pricing Configuration">
    Set up billing by connecting your Stripe account.

    - **Select Country**
    - Click **Connect** to link your Stripe account  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency3.png" alt="Pricing Setup" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer Management">
    Add and manage your customers here.  
    Each added customer (User B) will access the platform under your agency configuration.

    - Click **Add Customer**
    - Enter customer details  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency4.png" alt="Customer Management" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Module Access Control">
    Enable the modules you want to provide access to your customers.

    Available modules include:
    - Actions  
    - Visibility  
    - Integrations  
    - Assistants  
    - Billing  
    - Call  
    - AI Inbox  
    - Campaigns  
    - Chat Assistant  
    - Customers  
    - Emails  
    - Knowledge Base  
    - Phone Numbers  
    - SMS AI Agent  
    - User and Controls  
    - Users  
    - Voice AI Agent  
    - Assistant Template  
    - Email AI Agent  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency5.png" alt="Module Access Control" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Additional Settings">
    Configure support and email preferences.

    - **ContactSwing Help Center**: Enable if support access is required  
    - **Email Configuration**: Set up outbound email settings  

    <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/helpsessionvideos/Contactswing/images/Agency/agency6.png" alt="Additional Settings" />
  </Step>

</Steps>

**Notes:**
- All branding, domain, and pricing are fully controlled by the agency.
- Customers access the platform using the agency’s configured domain and branding.
- Module permissions can be updated at any time.
